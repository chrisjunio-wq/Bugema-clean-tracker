Frequently asked questions.

Need help with an issue? Start by sending a report so the service team can review it.

Report a problem →
What can I report?

You can report waste and cleanliness problems in the Bugema community and provide the location and details.

How do I track a report I submitted?

Open Track a Report and enter the report number and the name used when you submitted it.

What do the progress steps mean?

The checklist shows when a report is submitted, assigned to a team, in progress, and resolved. Steps are checked as the administrator updates the report.

Who can see my report details?

Report details are used by the responsible service team to review and address the issue. Tracking requires your report number and the name used at submission.
