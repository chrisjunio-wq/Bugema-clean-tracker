About the service area

Bugema Clean Community Tracker supports waste reports at Bugema University and nearby villages, trading centres, markets and roadside service areas. Include a clear landmark so administrators can direct each report to the appropriate campus or community cleaning team.

Contact and urgent concerns

This tracker is for community waste-management reports. For immediate danger or urgent health and safety concerns, contact the relevant local authority or emergency service directly.
